Welcome, and thanks for stopping by. This is the first post on the blog, so
consider it the front door rather than a deep dive.

## Who I am

I'm Hoa, a cloud engineer. I spend my days wrangling infrastructure: cloud
platforms, platform engineering, infrastructure-as-code, and the glue that holds
it all together. A lot of what I learn lives in scattered notes and
half-remembered fixes. This blog is my attempt to write it down properly.

## What to expect

Short, practical posts on the things I actually run into:

- AWS and cloud architecture
- DevOps practices and CI/CD pipelines
- Platform engineering and developer tooling
- Terraform and infrastructure-as-code
- The occasional war story when something breaks at 3am

No filler, no listicles, just the notes I wish I'd had when I hit the problem.

## More to come

This is the beginning. New posts are on the way, so check back soon. If you want
to follow along or reach out, my links are at the top of the page.
